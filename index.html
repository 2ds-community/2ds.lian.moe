了解我的理念 My Philosophy

设计理念 Design Philosophy

少即是多。每一行代码都有它存在的理由。 Less is more. Every line of code has its reason to exist.

◯

简约 Simplicity

去掉一切不必要的装饰，让内容自己说话。留白不是空洞，而是呼吸的空间。 Strip away all unnecessary decoration and let the content speak for itself. White space is not emptiness — it is room to breathe.

◆

高雅 Elegance

精心挑选的字体、克制的配色、恰到好处的动效。细节之处见品味。 Carefully chosen typography, restrained colors, and just the right amount of motion. Taste lives in the details.

⬡

模块化 Modularity

每个组件独立可复用，像积木一样自由组合。代码整洁，结构清晰。 Each component is independent and reusable, freely composable like building blocks. Clean code, clear structure.
